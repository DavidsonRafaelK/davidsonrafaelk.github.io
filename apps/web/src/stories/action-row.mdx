<Subtitle>Calls to action, never a bare button</Subtitle>

`ActionRow` takes a list of button definitions and renders them with a lowercase
"or" between each pair. That word is the whole point. It turns a row of controls
into an offer, which is the difference between a toolbar and a conversation.

## The useless option

Several sections offer a second choice that does nothing on purpose. It is part
of the site's voice, not an unfinished feature, and removing it would make the
page read like a product landing page.

<Canvas of={ActionRowStories.WithAJoke} />

A button with no `href` and no `action` renders as an inert `<button>`. That is
intentional and should stay that way.

## Colour discipline

Each button carries its own `boxColor`, and sections pick distinct hues so no
two adjacent rows repeat. When you add a section, look at what the sections
above and below it use before choosing.
