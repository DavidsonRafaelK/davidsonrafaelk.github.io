`@homepage/ui/skiper-ui` holds the components that exist to be looked at. They
are not general-purpose building blocks and should not be treated as such.

The rule is one per section at most, placed as a full-bleed break after the
content rather than inside the reading column. Two in a row and the page starts
competing with itself.

## HoverExpand

Closes the projects section. Hovering a panel expands it while the rest
contract. It takes `{ src, alt, code }` and the site feeds it `projectsData`.

<Canvas of={ShowpieceStories.HoverExpand} />

<Source
	code={`<HoverExpand_001
  images={projectsData.map((p) => ({
    src: p.imageUrl ?? "",
    alt: p.title,
    code: p.title,
  }))}
/>`}
	language="tsx"
/>

## CutoutCard

A compound component: `CutoutCardMedia`, `CutoutCardContent`, `CutoutCardFooter`
and friends, with `CutoutCorner` cutting the inverted radius that gives it its
name. `ProjectCard` composes them and picks six projects at random per render.

The date stamp in `CutoutCardInsetLabel` is where the site's micro-label style
comes from, and the project pages reuse it.

<Canvas of={ShowpieceStories.CutoutCards} />

## ProgressiveBlur

A fixed gradient mask pinned to the top or bottom of the viewport, softening
where content meets the edge. It is the one component in the codebase allowed to
carry a hardcoded hex, because it has to match the page background exactly
rather than track a token that might shift.

<Canvas of={ShowpieceStories.ProgressiveBlurBottom} />

<Source
	code={`<ProgressiveBlur position="bottom" backgroundColor="#f5f4f3" height="110px" />`}
	language="tsx"
/>

In the story above it is given `className="absolute"` so it stays inside the
frame. On a real page it is `fixed` and belongs at the root, outside the panel.

## A gotcha worth remembering

Once UI's own notes flag this one, and it applies to every full-bleed section
here: `overflow="hidden"` clips gradients into hard bands unless the parent has
a radius. The homepage gets away with it because the crowd canvas wrapper is
`rounded-b-3xl`.
